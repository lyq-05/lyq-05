## 边做边学 · Learning by making

- **Agent 协作 / Working with agents** — 把需求说清楚，拆分任务，核对结果，再继续改进。<br>Describe the problem, break down the work, review the result, and iterate.
- **产品体验 / Product experience** — 从自己遇到的小问题出发，关注真正使用时的感受。<br>Start with small, real problems and pay attention to how a product feels in use.
- **工程实践 / Engineering practice** — 在 [CareerFlow](https://github.com/lyq-05/careerflow) 中探索从开发到交付的完整流程。<br>Explore the development-to-delivery workflow through CareerFlow.
